Questions guests ask

How do I book?

Send us a booking request from the stay's page, or email us with your dates and number of guests. We reply with the total price and availability, and confirm once payment is done.

When do I pay?

After we confirm your dates. We share the total for your stay and the payment details by email.

How does check-in work?

All our homes have self check-in. Before you arrive we send the exact address, directions and how to get the keys.

Can I book more than one home?

Yes. Cozy Tapovan and Tranquil are in the same building, and so are the two Doon Square flats, which works well for families and groups. Mention it in your request.

Are long stays possible?

Yes. Tell us your dates and we'll share a price for weekly or monthly stays.

Can I cook?

Every home has a kitchen with cookware, so you can cook your own meals.
