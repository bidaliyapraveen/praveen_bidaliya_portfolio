About this home

Relax with the whole family at this peaceful place to stay in the Garhwal hills, close to the villages of Santudhar, Satpuli, Kirkhu and Patisain. Evenings are for the fire pit and the outdoor dining area, and breakfast is included, with a kitchen for your own meals too.
