About this home

Bring the whole family to The Amelie Villa, a cozy 1BHK homestay in the peaceful Isoti village of Pauri Garhwal. Surrounded by lush mountains and fresh air, the villa offers spacious rooms with attached bathrooms, a huge terrace and a beautiful garden with a porch to relax and enjoy nature. It's an off-beat getaway for travellers looking for silence, scenic views and a true Himalayan village experience, with lots of room for fun.
