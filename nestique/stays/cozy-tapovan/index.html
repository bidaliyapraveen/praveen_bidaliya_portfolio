About this home

Cozy Tapovan is set up for slow mornings: soft light, plenty of plants, a kitchen you can actually cook in and a living room made for doing nothing. There's a canopy reading nook, a bedroom with full-length windows and a balcony strung with fairy lights for the evenings. Tapovan's cafés and yoga schools are close by.
