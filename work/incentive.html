Simplifying a complex incentive system

Every month, sales teams run different incentive schemes. One scheme might be based on sales volume. Another might depend on a product, target, performance slab or a special booster. As the rules increase, creating a scheme becomes harder.

This project was about making that process easier for the people who manage these schemes.

Deep dive

I studied complex, real-world incentive schemes in detail to understand how they are structured, how different rules and conditions work, and how payouts are calculated. This helped me identify common patterns, dependencies, and pain points across different schemes.

Collaborative exploration

I brainstormed with business teams and stakeholders to understand how incentives are structured today, uncover pain points, and identify opportunities to simplify and improve the experience.

What I found

There were many schemes, but most followed similar building blocks.

Breaking Down the Complexity

This was the turning point. Instead of designing for hundreds of schemes, I identified reusable modules.

Design Goal

I wanted to make incentive creation:

✅ Simple • ✅ Flexible • ✅ Guided • ✅ Reusable

Product Experience

I designed a unified experience that supports different incentive structures through reusable modules. A guided workflow and live summary help users configure complex schemes with clarity and confidence.

Final Output

The final solution unifies the entire incentive creation process into a single, guided platform. Users can create different types of incentive schemes, configure complex business rules, define rewards, handle exceptions, review the configuration, and publish confidently—all without switching between different systems or workflows.

AI-powered scheme creation

I introduced an AI-powered way to create incentive schemes from scratch. Teams can build an entire scheme through an AI assistant, while the system continuously checks the configuration, identifies errors, and suggests improvements based on previous schemes. This helps teams create accurate schemes faster, with less manual effort.

Impact & Learnings

This project changed my approach to enterprise product design. Initially, the problem looked like managing many different incentive schemes. Through research and analysis, I realised the real problem was managing complexity.

The biggest insight was that users don't need simpler business rules. They need simpler ways to work with complex business rules.

By identifying common patterns and designing reusable building blocks, we created a flexible system that can adapt to different business needs while keeping the experience intuitive.
