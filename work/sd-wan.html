Enterprise SD-WAN, reimagined for clarity

Lavelle Networks is an enterprise SD-WAN platform that connects distributed users, locations, and cloud applications using intelligent network routing. Their product serves telecom operators, enterprises, and ISPs across India.

The existing dashboard was built for engineers — dense, graph-heavy, and difficult to navigate for the range of personas actually using the product. The redesign needed to serve everyone from CIOs to helpdesk teams, without losing depth for power users.

Business goals - Creating a Unified and Intelligent Monitoring Experience

The business aimed to:

  • Improve enterprise customer experience
  • Simplify operational workflows
  • Increase platform usability
  • Reduce issue resolution effort
  • Enable proactive monitoring and alerting
  • Build a scalable enterprise platform for future AI capabilities

The Challenge - Managing Enterprise Networks Was Complex and Operationally Heavy

Enterprise users managing SD-WAN infrastructure faced multiple challenges:

  • Difficult network visibility
  • Fragmented monitoring workflows
  • Slow troubleshooting processes
  • High dependency on operational teams
  • Cognitive overload from data-heavy interfaces

The existing experience lacked clarity, scalability, and proactive intelligence, making it difficult for users to quickly identify and resolve network issues.

Timelines

A 4-week discovery phase combined qualitative user interviews, stakeholder alignment sessions, workflow shadowing, and competitive benchmarking.

Research & Analysis - Understanding before designing

Stakeholder Interviews

6 sessions with product, engineering, and sales leaders to align on business goals and current pain points impacting retention.

User Interviews.

12 sessions across 5 user roles admins, operators, executives, and helpdesk revealing role-specific needs and failure patterns.

Workflow Analysis

Task analysis of 8 core monitoring and configuration workflows to identify friction, dead-ends, and redundant steps in current flows.

Competitive Analysis

Deep analysis of Cisco vManage, VMware SASE, and Fortinet SD-WAN for IA patterns, KPI hierarchies, and role-specific features.

Key insight

What research revealed

Visibility Was the Biggest Problem

Users could not quickly identify network anomalies or performance degradation.

Monitoring Required Too Much Effort

Critical actions were buried under multiple layers of navigation.

Users Needed Faster Decision-Making

The platform lacked proactive intelligence and contextual guidance.

Information Architecture Was Fragmented

Monitoring, analytics, alerts, and troubleshooting existed in disconnected flows.

Redesigned navigation structure

Regrouped from a flat, API-driven IA to a task-oriented, role-aware hierarchy. 6 top-level sections with clear child groupings.

Outcomes & Impact

Measurable improvements across every role

Simplified Navigation

Task-based IA replaced flat menus. Users find what they need in under 10 seconds.

Faster Incident Response

Operators resolve critical alerts 40% faster with direct-to-diagnostic navigation.

Executive Visibility

CIOs can assess network health in under 30 seconds — no engineering translation needed.

Operational Monitoring

Helpdesk teams manage incidents end-to-end without escalating to engineering for basic diagnostics.

Learnings

Key Takeaways

This project reinforced the importance of:

  • Balancing business goals with operational usability
  • Designing scalable enterprise systems
  • Simplifying complex technical workflows
  • Using AI as an assistive layer rather than a replacement
  • Prioritizing discoverability in high-frequency workflows

Final Reflect

Designing Beyond Dashboards

This project was not just about creating dashboards, it was about simplifying enterprise operations, enabling faster decision-making, and building a scalable monitoring ecosystem that could evolve with future intelligence capabilities.
